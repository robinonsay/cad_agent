An enclosure for a 90 x 45 mm perfboard with a mounting hole in each corner. A Raspberry Pi Pico 2 lies along the board with its micro-USB port exposed through one short end, and three 3.5 mm jacks sit in a row along one long edge of the board, exposed through that long side of the enclosure. Cables must plug in with the lid on. The enclosure is a base and a removable lid, printed on an FDM printer.
