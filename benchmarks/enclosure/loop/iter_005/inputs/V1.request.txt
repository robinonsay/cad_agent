An enclosure for an 80 x 60 mm perfboard with a mounting hole in each corner. A Raspberry Pi Pico 2 and one 3.5 mm jack sit on the board, and both the Pico's micro-USB port and the jack must be exposed through the same short end of the enclosure so cables plug in with the lid on. The enclosure is a base and a removable lid, printed on an FDM printer.
