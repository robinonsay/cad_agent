# G04 Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W case: requirements (L1 / L2 / L3)

A generalisation design for the OpenSCAD assembly agent, modelled on E1-01
(`benchmarks/enclosure/requirements.md`): a catalogue board placed from datums, a base and a lid,
standoffs at the board's hole pattern, wall openings sized from plug envelopes, interference and
clearance checks against reference models and keep-out volumes. The machine-readable request (L1
only, `dev.json` format) is `design.json` next to this file. How a blind judge applies this document
is in section 7.

**Plan brief (verbatim):** "A two-part case for a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, held at its four 2.75 mm
holes on the 58 x 23 mm pattern. I need openings for the mini-HDMI, the two micro-USB ports (data
and power) and the microSD card slot, each with room for the plug body. Lid goes on with M2.5
screws."

**How to read this.** L1 says what the owner needs, L2 turns it into an engineering requirement
with numbers, L3 is a concrete check Python can run on the delivered STLs. Every row has a method
(inspection, analysis, demonstration, test, human, as in `design.json`), a pass/fail criterion with a
number and a tolerance, and a weight (3 = critical, 2 = major, 1 = minor). A `G<n>` after the weight
marks a gate: a failed gate makes the design WRONG (section 5). Every number that comes from an
assumption (section 3) is provisional until the owner confirms it. Do not relax a criterion because
the agent fails it.

## 1. Frames, tolerances and the two interference checks

### 1.1 Frames

- **Case frame = board frame:** X along the board's 65 mm side, Y along its 30 mm side, Z up. The
  origin is the board's PCB min corner with **Z = 0 at the PCB underside**, so the PCB occupies Z 0 to
  1.4 (this is the catalogue `rpi_zero2w` module frame). The mini-HDMI and both micro-USB ports are on
  the **-Y** long wall, the microSD slot on the **-X** short wall, the GPIO footprint on +Y (D1).
- A design delivered mirrored or rotated by multiples of 90 degrees is normalised into this frame
  before grading (the judge records the transform); that is never penalised.

### 1.2 Measurement tolerances

All measurements are made by Python on the delivered meshes, never by eye.

| Quantity | Tolerance e | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| length, distance, clearance | 0.02 mm (mesh tessellation) | a value passes if it meets the limit to within e |
| angle | 0.5 degrees | same |
| interference volume | 0.01 mm3 | "no interference" means intersection volume <= 0.01 mm3 |
| hole diameter | largest inscribed circle of the hole's section (what a screw sees) | |
| boss / standoff OD | smallest enclosing circle of the section | |

### 1.3 The two interference checks

- **`clear_of(bodies, volume, clearance)`**: the minimum distance between every printed body in
  `bodies` and `volume` is >= `clearance`. Reported value: the signed minimum distance d (negative =
  penetration; the penetration volume is reported too). Pass: d >= clearance - e.
- **`inside(volume, cavity, clearance)`**: `volume` lies entirely in `cavity` and every point of it
  is >= `clearance` from the cavity boundary. Reported value: the signed minimum distance. Pass: d >=
  clearance - e.

`bodies` is **ALL** (base and lid, closed), **BASE** or **LID**. A design never builds or edits the
reference volumes: the grader generates them independently from the catalogue and the declared pose.
The agent may generate the same volumes for its own checks, but its copies are not trusted.

**Designed contact for this design.** Parts that are meant to touch are excluded from every
interference and clearance check. For these pairs only penetration (> 0.01 mm3) counts, and a
distance of zero is a pass:
- the PCB underside resting on the four standoff tops, inside the V-SEAT-k columns;
- a board screw in its pilot hole, and its head on the PCB top face;
- a lid screw in its boss pilot, and its head on the lid;
- the lid lip against its locating faces at the fit L2.8.2 sets;
- the lid plate resting on the base rim and on the four boss tops;
- the microSD card in its slot (not modelled; the card zone is a keep-out, not a solid).

## 2. Reference models, envelopes and datums

### 2.1 Sources

- **catalogue:** `benchmarks/generalize/parts/rpi_zero2w/part.json` (the board),
  `benchmarks/enclosure/parts/plug_microusb_b/part.json`,
  `benchmarks/generalize/parts/plug_minihdmi/part.json` and
  `benchmarks/enclosure/parts/screw_m2p5_selftap/part.json`. These are the only place part numbers
  may live.
- **envelope:** a keep-out or access volume generated by Python from datums (offsets, sweeps,
  columns). Never drawn by hand.

| ID | Model | Check | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|

| R2 | M-PCB | hole pattern | dia 2.75 +/-0.1, pitch 58.0 x 23.0 +/-0.1 |

**Seating rule.** The RBA is placed at the pose declared in the layout, expressed in the design's
frame. The grader verifies it: `PCB_BOTTOM` lies on the standoff tops (distance <= 0.10) and
`HOLE_1..4` are coaxial with the pilot holes (least-squares residual <= 0.25). If the declaration is
missing or fails, the RBA is seated by that fit instead; with no standoffs it is centred in the
cavity 5.0 above the floor and every L1.3 check fails.

## 3. Assumptions (A) and fixed decisions (D)

Each A-row gives the value chosen and the uncertainty. **A1, A3, A5 and A7 matter most.**

| # | Question | Value chosen | Why / uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | Which walls do the ports face? | Where the board puts them: mini-HDMI, micro-USB data and micro-USB power all on one long edge (the **-Y** wall), microSD on the short edge at the SD end (the **-X** wall). | The connectors' places are fixed by the board's mechanical drawing. **Uncertainty: low.** |
| **A2** | Board and connector dimensions? | PCB 65 x 30, holes dia 2.75 on 58 x 23 (datasheet). PCB thickness 1.4, connector bodies and overhangs from `part.json` (HDMI 11.2 x 7.5 x 3.2, overhang 0.5; micro-USB 7.5 x 5.9 x 2.6, overhang 0.9; microSD cage 14.0 x 12.0 x 1.4, overhang 1.0; port centres 12.4, 41.4, 54.0 and SD 16.9). | Raspberry Pi mechanical drawing and vendor receptacle sheets. Thickness, depths and overhangs are approximate (+/-0.5). **Uncertainty: medium.** Measure one board. |
| **A3** | Plug sizes? | mini-HDMI overmold 17.0 x 8.0, shell 10.4 x 2.4, inserted 10.0; micro-USB overmold 10.0 x 7.0, shell 6.9 x 1.85, inserted 5.0. | Typical cables. Each overmold must enter its opening for the plug to seat, so plug envelopes, not fixed hole sizes, set the openings. **Uncertainty: medium** (HDMI overmolds vary 15 to 20 wide). Measure the owner's cables. |
| **A4** | What does "an opening for the microSD slot" have to allow? | A card access zone, 12.0 wide x 5.4 high (Y 10.9..22.9, Z 0.4..5.8), open through the -X wall from the cage outward, for the card (it protrudes 2.5 when seated) and a fingertip. | `part.json` `sd_card_zone`. **Uncertainty: medium.** |
| **A5** | Clearance above and below the board? | At least **5.0** above the PCB top (V-KO-TOP, Z 1.4..6.4; tallest top part is the HDMI receptacle, 4.6) and **2.0** below the PCB underside. No GPIO header is fitted. | A plain Zero 2 W is flat. **Uncertainty: medium.** A soldered 2 x 20 header would need at least 9 above. Confirm. |
| **A6** | Keep-out round a mounting hole? | Dia 6.0 (the Raspberry Pi mechanical drawing's keep-out round each hole). | **Uncertainty: low.** |
| **A7** | How is the board fastened? | 4 x M2.5 self-tapping screws through the 2.75 holes into printed standoffs (pilot dia 2.0 to 2.2, 5.0 minimum engagement, standoff OD 5.0 to 6.0). | M2.5 passes a 2.75 hole and the 5.0 head fits the 6.0 keep-out; the brief names M2.5 for the lid, and the same size is the obvious choice for the board. **Uncertainty: medium** (a design could locate the board by pins and let the lid clamp it, but then L1.3's pilot rows would not apply; no alternative is graded). |
| **A8** | Lid closure? | Fixed by D4 (flat lid with lip, 4 x M2.5). | The brief says "lid goes on with M2.5 screws". |
| A9 | Printing? | FDM, PLA or PETG, 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 layers, 200 x 200 x 200 bed. Sliding fit 0.15 to 0.35 per side. Board to wall at least 0.5. | Common printers. |
| A10 | Wall thickness? | Walls, floor and lid plate 2.0 to 4.0. | 5 perimeters at 0.4. |
| A11 | Maximum size? | Closed outside <= 90 x 40 x 22 (X x Y x Z). | A tight design is about 86 x 37 x 19. |
| A12 | Camera port, GPIO, LEDs, buttons, vents? | None needed. | The camera FFC and GPIO are unused; the Zero 2 W dissipates about 1 W. **Uncertainty: low; confirm.** |
| A13 | Cable pull and mass? | 0.2 N sideways pull at a port centre (a light cable's drag; the board is not clamped to the desk); mass = printed volume x 0.45 g/cm3 plus 10 g for the board. | Cable tugging on a desk; 0.45 is PLA at about 35% infill. **Uncertainty: medium.** |
| A14 | Which micro-USB is which? | Not graded: the two openings are interchangeable for the case. | The case does not care. |

| # | Fixed decision (a choice that would otherwise be ambiguous) |
|---|---|
| **D1** | Frame as in 1.1; the board sits in its module orientation, PCB underside at Z = 0, identity pose. |
| **D2** | The board is held by 4 M2.5 screws into 4 standoffs under the holes. The standoffs are the only thing touching the board. |
| **D3** | The lid is held by **4 further M2.5 screws** into 4 base bosses that lie **outside the board's outline** (>= 0.5 from the board and every connector). No fastener is shared with the board. |
| **D4** | **Lid form:** one flat plate covering the base's outer footprint (+/-0.5 on all four sides), 2.0 to 3.0 thick, with a continuous locating lip on its underside fitting inside the base walls. The lip may be notched only where a boss is. Engagement 2.0 to 3.0, fit 0.15 to 0.35 per side, total lid height (plate + lip) <= 6.0. Lid holes dia 2.8 to 3.0 over the bosses. No snap, sliding or deep cap lid. |
| **D5** | Opening sizes are **derived from plug envelopes**: each cabled-port opening must contain its V- envelope plus 0.3 and be no bigger than the envelope plus 1.5 on any side. |
| **D6** | The microSD opening is one rectangular opening in the -X wall around V-SD, no bigger than V-SD's section plus 1.5 on any side. |
| **D7** | Print orientation is stated in the layout. Default: base open side up, lid outer face down. |

## 7. Grading notes (design-specific rubric)

`benchmarks/generalize/rubric.md` did not exist when this file was written, so the grading
procedure is given here. It follows `benchmarks/enclosure/rubric.md` (recipes in its section 3) and
should become a pointer to the generic rubric when that exists.

1. **Package gate (G0).** Files in L2.13.1 exist and load, else DOES NOT MEET with one defect on
   L3.13.1.1.
2. **Load and units.** trimesh, then manifold3d `Manifold`; `status()` must be NoError; shells by
   `decompose()`. A largest axis outside 20 to 200 mm is a units defect; record, do not rescale.
3. **Normalise the frame** (1.1) with 90 degree rotations and mirroring only; record the transform.
4. **Build the references independently** (2.2, 2.3) from the catalogue at the declared pose, run
   sanity checks R1 to R7 and the seating rule, then every applicable L3 in requirement order.
   Record the measured value, the limit, the signed margin (negative = fail) and PASS, FAIL or
   UNVERIFIED. UNVERIFIED counts as FAIL; N/A only for the optional physical rows.
5. **Recipes.** `clear_of`, `inside`, apertures, wall thickness, hole sizes, overhang, bed contact,
   thin features and stability: `benchmarks/enclosure/rubric.md` sections 3.2 to 3.7 apply unchanged,
   with this design's V- volumes. Aperture regions are counted per wall slab with `decompose()`,
   ignoring regions under 1 mm3.
6. **Verdict.** WRONG: any gate fails. MINOR: no gate fails but a weight-3 L3 fails. CORRECT: no
   gate fails and every weight-3 L3 passes. **MEETS** = CORRECT and every applicable non-physical L3
   of weight 1 and 2 passes. Report also the weighted score and the worst normalised margin among
   weight-3 rows.
7. **Precedence.** Numbers in this document beat any number in a part.json. The plug-seating and SD
   path checks (L3.5.1.1, L3.6.1.1, L3.7.1.1) outrank everything about opening size. Designed
   contact (1.3) is never interference. No alternative is accepted for D3 and D4; an unlisted
   alternative is graded on the default numbers and noted as an observation for the owner.
8. **Where a defect is.** One entry per failed or UNVERIFIED L3 with `req`, `status`, `body`
   (base, lid, both, layout), `feature` (by function and SCAD name), `face` (surface and outward
   normal), `location` (mm, case frame, point or box), `measured` / `limit` / `margin`,
   `reference` (model or envelope and datum), `render`, `evidence`, and `needed` (a verifiable
   number, never a design instruction). Group L3s that fail for one geometric reason.

## Audit

Adversarial audit; the Feasibility design was rebuilt in manifold3d (base 1 shell, lid 1 shell, overlap 0, closed
box 85.8 x 36.6 x 18.9, volumes 16.7 and 6.9 cm3) and every gate checked against it. `rpi_zero2w`, `plug_minihdmi`,
`plug_microusb_b` and `screw_m2p5_selftap` load and pass `parts.validate` and `parts.gate`. L1 13 (in `design.json` too), L2 47, L3 71 recounted.

1. **Bridge limit (L2.10.1).** The Feasibility HDMI opening is 17.6 wide, so its roof is a 17.6 bridge, over the
   enclosure rubric's 15.0 limit: the design could not meet L3.10.1.1. A 45 degree roof cannot be used because the
   opening must contain the plug envelope plus 0.3 and stay inside envelope plus 1.5. Bridge limit set to 20.0 for this design
   (the widest opening D5 allows is 20.0).
2. **Tipping (A13, L2.11.3, Feasibility).** The Feasibility said 45 g; the rebuilt mass is 20.6 g, and a 0.5 N pull at the
   port height (10.4) gives 5.2 N.mm against 3.7 N.mm restoring, so the design tipped. Pull set to 0.2 N (assumption, medium uncertainty);
   Feasibility recomputed (margin 1.6 N.mm).
3. **L3.12.3.1** was a human judgement; replaced by a measurable band (axes 5.0 apart, 8 distinct).
4. **Lid bosses** were said to be "fused to the walls" but a dia 6.0 boss at 3.0 from each wall is tangent, not fused;
   they are fused to the floor (the base stays one shell). Wording fixed.
5. Checked, no change: all numbers in the request, L1.1 to L1.13, A-rows and `part.json` agree (65 x 30, 58 x 23, 2.75, 3.5 inset, connector sizes and overhangs, plug envelopes, 5.0 / 2.0 keep-outs, 0.5 clearance, 1.5 opening growth, 90 x 40 x 22);
   mouth to wall distances 0.7 / 0.3 in 0.2 to 1.5; USB ligament 2.0; pilot depth 6.6 at its limit; lip lowest point 6.5 against the 6.4 keep-out;
   the bosses clear the board by 1.0 or more and the SD zone; the request carries no number the judge needs that L1 omits (the L1 texts are given to the agent).
