# G38 Perfboard soldering holder: requirements (L1 / L2 / L3)

A generalisation design for the OpenSCAD assembly agent, modelled on E1-01
(`benchmarks/enclosure/requirements.md`): a catalogue perfboard placed from datums, pins and seats
at the board's holes, a keep-out volume under the board, and interference and clearance checks
against reference models. It adds a tilting cradle on two bolts (a rotation sweep) and a stability
calculation. The machine-readable request (L1 only, `dev.json` format) is `design.json` next to
this file. How a blind judge applies this document is in section 7.

**Plan brief (verbatim):** "A tilting holder for a 70 x 50 mm perfboard so I can solder it upside
down, with four corner pins at the board's 3 mm holes and a 10 mm height keep-out under the board for
component bodies. The base is heavy enough not to tip at a 20 degree tilt and prints flat."

**How to read this.** L1 says what the owner needs, L2 turns it into an engineering requirement with
numbers, L3 is a concrete check Python can run on the delivered STLs. Every row has a method
(inspection, analysis, demonstration, test, human, as in `design.json`), a pass/fail criterion with a
number and a tolerance, and a weight (3 = critical, 2 = major, 1 = minor). A `G<n>` after the weight
marks a gate: a failed gate makes the design WRONG (section 5). Every number that comes from an
assumption (section 3) is provisional until the owner confirms it. Do not relax a criterion because
the agent fails it.

## 1. Frames, tolerances and the checks

### 1.1 Frames

- **Holder frame (zero tilt):** X along the board's 70 mm side, Y along its 50 mm side, Z up. The
  origin is the board's min-X, min-Y corner on its **component side (underside)**. The board occupies
  **Z 0 to 1.6**: Z = 0 is the component side, facing down; Z = 1.6 is the solder side, facing up. The
  tilt axis is parallel to X (D2). The STLs are delivered in this zero-tilt pose.
- **Tilt angle theta** is the rotation of the cradle (and everything fixed to it) about the pivot
  axis, right-handed about +X, in degrees. The base never moves. The judge tests both senses.
- A design delivered mirrored or rotated by multiples of 90 degrees is normalised into this frame
  before grading (the judge records the transform); that is never penalised.

### 1.2 Measurement tolerances

All measurements are made by Python on the delivered meshes, never by eye.

| Quantity | Tolerance e | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| length, distance, clearance | 0.02 mm (mesh tessellation) | a value passes if it meets the limit to within e |
| angle | 0.5 degrees | same |
| interference volume | 0.01 mm3 | "no interference" means intersection volume <= 0.01 mm3 |
| hole diameter | largest inscribed circle of the hole's section | |
| post / seat / pin OD | smallest enclosing circle of the section | |

### 1.3 The checks

- **`clear_of(bodies, volume, clearance)`**: the minimum distance between every printed body in
  `bodies` and `volume` is >= `clearance`; reported as the signed minimum distance d (negative =
  penetration, with the volume). Pass: d >= clearance - e.
- **`tilt(theta)`**: rotate the CRADLE, the board, M-BOLT ends fixed in the cradle and every
  cradle-fixed envelope rigidly about the pivot axis by theta; the BASE stays.
- **`first_contact(sense)`**: the smallest |theta| in that sense at which the rotated CRADLE
  penetrates the BASE by more than 0.01 mm3, found in 0.5 degree steps to a limit of 45 degrees.

`bodies` is **ALL** (base and cradle), **BASE** or **CRADLE**. A design never builds or edits the
reference volumes: the grader generates them independently from the catalogue and the declared
layout.

**Designed contact for this design.** Parts that are meant to touch are excluded from every
interference and clearance check; for these only penetration (> 0.01 mm3) counts, and a distance of
zero is a pass:
- the board's underside resting on the four seat tops, inside the V-SEAT-k columns;
- a pin in its board hole (clearance 0.05 to 0.15 is intended, so a touch is allowed);
- an M3 bolt in the cradle's pilot hole (thread forming) and the bolt head on the upright's outer face;
- the bolt shank in the upright's clearance hole (a touch is allowed).

The cradle and the base are **not** designed to touch each other anywhere: a touch is a defect
(L2.4.3).

## 2. Reference models, envelopes and datums

### 2.1 Sources

- **catalogue:** `benchmarks/enclosure/parts/perfboard/part.json` (generic board, sized from the
  request) and `benchmarks/generalize/parts/bolt_m3_iso4762/part.json`. These are the only places
  part numbers may live.

with the pins (least-squares residual <= 0.15). If the declaration is missing or fails, M-PERF is
seated by that fit instead.

| # | Question | Value chosen | Why / uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|

| **A2** | What are the board's holes? | **Dia 3.0 at 2.5 mm in from each edge** (pitch 65.0 x 45.0): "the board's 3 mm holes" and the catalogue family default. | **A3** | How tall are the component bodies? | 10.0 mm below the board's component side, over the whole footprint except the seat columns, **at every tilt** and for **both** printed bodies. | Taken from the request. **Uncertainty: medium** (an electrolytic capacitor or a header can be taller). |
| **A4** | Pins and seats? | Pin dia 2.7 to 2.9 (0.1 to 0.3 under the hole), its top 1.0 to 3.0 above the solder side. Seat (post) OD 5.0 to 7.0, tops at Z = 0 within 0.10; the board's underside rests on the seat tops only. | Locates the board without a fit that jams. **Uncertainty: low.** |
| **A5** | How open must the solder side be? | Nothing printed above the solder-side plane inside the board outline except the pins (V-OPEN), at zero tilt and at +/-20 degrees. | Iron and hand access, and the board lifts straight off the pins. **Uncertainty: low.** |

Confirm. |
| **A7** | Pivot hardware? | M3 bolts (catalogue), length declared in the layout. Base upright holes dia 3.3 to 3.5; cradle pilots dia 2.4 to 2.6 (tap drill 2.5) at least 6.0 deep; engagement 5.0 or more and not past the pilot floor; uprights at least 3.0 thick; 0.5 mm or more between each cradle end face and its upright (the same 0.5 as L1.4). | ISO 273 and 261 values in the catalogue part. **Uncertainty: low.** |
| **A8** | How is "heavy enough not to tip" measured? | Mass = printed volume x **0.45 g/cm3** (PLA at about 35% infill) + board with parts **30 g** (about 10 g bare plus 20 g of components) + 1 g per bolt. | **A10** | Maximum size? | Holder at zero tilt <= 120 x 100 x 50 (X x Y x Z). | A tight design is about 110 x 90 x 40. |
| **A11** | Strength? | No printed feature thinner than 2.0 except the pins; cradle bars at least 5.0 in both section dimensions; at least 2.0 of material round every bolt and pilot hole. | 5 perimeters. |
| A12 | Magnets, clips, a tilt scale? | Not required. | The plan lists a magnet capability for this design, but the brief does not mention a magnet; nothing is graded for it. |

|---|---|
| **D1** | Frames as in 1.1; the cradle's zero tilt is the board horizontal. |
| **D2** | Exactly **two printed bodies**: a base (a heavy plate with two upright walls) and a cradle (the pins, seats and a frame, carrying the board). The only other parts are the two M3 pivot bolts, along X. |

| **D4** | The cradle's frame lies **outside the board outline in plan** (at least 0.5 from every board edge), so the volume under the board is free of it; only the four seat posts stand under the board. |
| **D5** | The base's stated print orientation is the delivered one (Z up, underside on the bed). |
| **D6** | Pins are part of the cradle body. Seats are the cradle's four posts. |
| **D7** | Tilt sign: right-handed about +X; both senses are graded equally. |

## 6. Feasibility

A concrete design that meets every row exists. Holder frame, mm.

## 7. Grading notes (design-specific rubric)

`benchmarks/generalize/rubric.md` did not exist when this file was written, so the grading
procedure is given here. It follows `benchmarks/enclosure/rubric.md` (recipes in its section 3) and
should become a pointer to the generic rubric when that exists.

1. **Package gate (G0).** Files in L2.12.1 exist and load, else DOES NOT MEET with one defect on
   L3.12.1.1.
2. **Load and units.** trimesh, then manifold3d `Manifold`; `status()` must be NoError; shells by
   `decompose()`. A largest axis outside 20 to 200 mm is a units defect; record, do not rescale.
3. **Normalise the frame** (1.1) with 90 degree rotations and mirroring only; record the transform.
4. **Build the references independently** from the catalogue at the declared layout, run R1 to R4
   and the seating rule, then every applicable L3 in requirement order. Record the measured value,
   the limit, the signed margin (negative = fail) and PASS, FAIL or UNVERIFIED. UNVERIFIED counts as
   FAIL; N/A only for the optional physical rows.
5. **Recipes.** Tilt: `Manifold.rotate` of the cradle and its envelopes about the pivot line (translate
   to the axis, rotate about X, translate back); `(a ^ b).volume()` for penetration and
   `min_gap` for distance. First contact: scan 0.5 degree steps. Stability: centre of mass from
   volumes at the A8 density (rotate the cradle and the board mass point with theta); the support
   polygon is the convex hull of the base's faces on the table (faces with normal -Z at the lowest Z);
   push moments about each hull edge. Overhang, bed contact, thin features, wall thickness, tool
   columns: `benchmarks/enclosure/rubric.md` sections 3.2 to 3.7.
6. **Verdict.** WRONG: any gate fails. MINOR: no gate fails but a weight-3 L3 fails. CORRECT: no
   gate fails and every weight-3 L3 passes. **MEETS** = CORRECT and every applicable non-physical L3
   of weight 1 and 2 passes. Report also the weighted score and the worst normalised margin among
   weight-3 rows.
7. **Precedence.** Numbers in this document beat any number in a part.json, in particular the board's
   hole numbers (D8). Designed contact (1.3) is never interference. A design that meets an L2's
   intent another way is graded on the default numbers and noted as an observation for the owner.
8. **Where a defect is.** One entry per failed or UNVERIFIED L3 with `req`, `status`, `body` (base,
   cradle, both, layout), `feature` (by function and SCAD name), `face` (surface and outward normal),
   `location` (mm, holder frame, and the theta at which it occurs), `measured` / `limit` / `margin`,
   `reference` (model or envelope and datum), `render`, `evidence`, and `needed` (a verifiable number,
   never a design instruction). Group L3s that fail for one geometric reason.

## Audit

Adversarial audit. The Feasibility numbers were re-derived by hand (sweep corner at 20 degrees: Z = -21.75 against the base top
at -25; keep-out corner -18.4; masses 55 + 7.4 + 30 + 2 g; push 23 against 42 N.mm; cradle bed contact about 40%; post-to-rail
contact 19 mm2). `perfboard` (enclosure catalogue) and `bolt_m3_iso4762` load and pass `parts.validate` and `parts.gate`. L1 12, L2 34, L3 53 recounted
(`design.json` has 12).

1. **Ambiguous request.** "2.5 mm in from each corner" can be read diagonally; the request now says "from each edge" (A2's meaning).
2. **Hidden numbers.** L1.6 gave the judge a 5.0 mm margin, a 0.5 N push, a 50% base mass fraction, 0.45 g/cm3 and a 30 g board that the
   agent was never told; they are now in L1.6. L1.5 now states the 5.0 mm engagement and L1.1 the pin diameter (2.7 to 2.9) and height
   (1.0 to 3.0). L1 texts are what the agent receives, so every number a gate or weight-3 row uses is now in the request or an L1.
3. **Contradictory gap.** L1.4 and L3.4.2.2 need 0.5 clearance at every step (which includes theta = 0, where the end-face gap sits), but A7 and
   L3.4.3.1 allowed 0.4. Both now say 0.5. The Feasibility gap was exactly 0.5 (no margin); uprights moved 0.1 outward (X -13.1..-9.1 and 79.1..83.1, gap 0.6) and the
   M3 x 10 tips now sit at -3.1 and 73.1, engagement 5.4 (still 5.0 to 6.0).
4. **Pivot-height band** was written relative to two datums ("+2 above SOLDER_SIDE, -15 below COMP_SIDE"); stated as absolute Z (+3.6 to -15.0), same numbers.
5. **Catalogue trap, no change.** The `perfboard` part holds a photo specimen for 70 x 50 (holes dia 2.0 at 2.0) that would replace the dia 3.0 / 2.5 family default;
   D8 already says the request's numbers win, and the request gives 3 mm and 2.5 mm explicitly. Left as a deliberate difficulty.
