An alarm box that holds an Arduino Nano and a 12 mm piezo buzzer. The Nano sits in the base on its four mounting holes with its mini-USB port at one short end, and that end stays open so a mini-USB plug can be pushed in, and the lid can still slide off with the cable attached. The base has a dovetail rail along each long wall; the lid slides on along the long axis with 0.25 mm of fit clearance and stops against the far end wall. A ring of sound holes in the lid sits over the buzzer, and the buzzer's own sound port must not be covered. Printed on an FDM printer in PLA without supports, and the closed box should be no larger than 80 x 40 x 30 mm.
