An L-shaped bracket for a NEMA 17 stepper motor (42.3 mm square body, 40 mm long, 5 mm shaft). The vertical face takes the motor on its 31 mm bolt pattern, with a 22.5 mm pilot register, and the shaft end passes through the face toward the side the flange is on. The horizontal flange has two M4 holes 40 mm apart on a line parallel to the bend. The motor weighs 0.35 kg and the bracket should survive about 20 N at the shaft. All screws are ISO 4762 socket-head cap screws, M3 for the motor and M4 for the flange, which I have in the stock lengths 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20 and 25 mm. Print it in PLA on an FDM printer, one piece, oriented so the layers do not split at the bend.
