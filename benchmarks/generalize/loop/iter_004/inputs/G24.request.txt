A clamp that grips a 2020 aluminium extrusion with one M5 button-head bolt into a drop-in T-nut, and that presents a 1/4-20 tripod stud on top for a small camera whose base is about 28 mm across. The clamp has a tongue that runs in the extrusion's T-slot so it slides along the slot until the bolt is tightened. The 1/4-20 stud has a hex head held captive in a pocket so it cannot turn, with 5.5 to 8 mm of thread standing proud of the top. The stud boss must carry a 3 N m torque without cracking, and the clamp must not twist on the extrusion at that torque. A hex key must be able to reach the M5 bolt. It is one printed part in PLA without supports, no larger than 60 x 30 x 30 mm.
