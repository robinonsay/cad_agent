A duct that connects a 60 x 60 x 25 mm fan (mounting holes on a 50 mm square pitch) to a 50 x 20 mm heatsink that is 30 mm tall. The fan sits on top and blows straight down; the heatsink stands below it with its fins running vertically, so the heatsink's leading face is its 50 x 20 mm top face and the duct meets it there. The air goes through a smoothly changing section from the fan to the heatsink and enters the fins with side seals of 1 mm at most (the duct's outlet slips over the top of the heatsink and the gap to its sides stays between 0.2 and 1 mm). Four M4 socket-head bolts hold the fan, through the fan's holes into the duct, and I need to reach those four screws with a hex driver from above. One printed duct on an FDM printer in PLA without supports, wall at least 1.6 mm.
