A tray for six M6 hotend nozzles (hex 7 mm across flats, 12 mm tall, 0.3 mm clearance per side in the pocket), a 7 mm combination wrench (120 x 14 x 4 mm, same clearance) in its own pocket, and four 6 x 3 mm disc magnets in pockets underneath that keep the tray on a steel surface, one in each quarter of the tray and spread over at least half its length and a quarter of its width. Loaded, the tray's centre of mass must lie at least 2 mm inside the four magnets (PLA 1.24 g/cm3, nozzles 3 g each, wrench 45 g, magnets 0.64 g each). The six nozzle pockets are 6 to 10 mm deep and stand in a regular row or grid (centres within 0.1 mm of the lattice) with at least 3 mm between neighbours, so the nozzles can be picked out one at a time with the fingers. Print the tray flat on an FDM printer without supports. It is no larger than 140 x 40 x 15 mm.
