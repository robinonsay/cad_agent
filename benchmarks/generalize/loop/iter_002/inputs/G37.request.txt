A carrier plate for a NEMA 17 stepper motor (42.3 mm square body, 40 mm long, 31 mm bolt pattern, 22 mm pilot), held by four ISO 4762 M3 socket-head screws (I have stock lengths 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20 and 25 mm), with the shaft passing through the plate and the motor behind the plate. A 16 tooth, module 1.5 pinion sits on the motor shaft and drives a 32 tooth idler gear that turns on a 608 bearing; both gears are 8 mm wide. Set the idler's centre distance from the gear parameters plus 0.2 mm, and keep a 0.5 mm gap between the gears and the plate. The motor's peak torque is 0.4 N m. The gears, motor and bearing are bought parts; the carrier is one part printed in PLA on an FDM printer.
