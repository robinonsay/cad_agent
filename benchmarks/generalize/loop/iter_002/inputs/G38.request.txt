A tilting holder for a 70 x 50 mm perfboard so I can solder it upside down, component side facing down. The board has a 3 mm hole whose centre is 2.5 mm in from each of the two edges at its corner, and four corner pins go through those holes. Nothing may come within 10 mm below the board, where the component bodies hang, and the solder side must be open from above. The board sits in a cradle that tilts on two M3 bolts through the uprights of a heavy base, at least 20 degrees each way. The base is heavy enough that the holder does not tip at a 20 degree tilt, and it prints flat on an FDM printer without supports.
