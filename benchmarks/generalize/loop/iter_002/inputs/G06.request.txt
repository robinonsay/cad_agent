A desk clock case built around a 1602 LCD module (PCB 80 x 36 mm, viewport 64.5 x 14 mm, four 2.5 mm mounting holes) and a Raspberry Pi Pico 2. The LCD is tilted 30 degrees from the table, so the front face of the case is a sloped face with a window for the LCD, and the module is fixed on its four mounting holes. The Pico 2 is mounted behind and below the LCD, partly under it, and its micro-USB port must open through the back wall so a cable plugs in with the case closed. The case is a base and a removable sloped front panel, printed on an FDM printer without supports, and it stands on a desk.
