A case for an Arduino Uno R3 (68.6 x 53.3 mm, four 3.2 mm mounting holes) that sits on four standoffs at the board's own mounting holes. The USB-B receptacle and the 5.5 mm barrel jack are on the same short edge of the board; both must be pluggable with the lid on and their cables attached, so each plug body (the moulded handle as well as the metal) has to fit through the wall. Put a row of ventilation slots in the lid over the ATmega328P, and screw the lid down with four M3 self-tapping screws. The case is a base and a removable lid, printed on an FDM printer in PLA without supports, and the board has to come out for service.
