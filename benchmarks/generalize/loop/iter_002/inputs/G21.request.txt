A heavy-duty mount for an MG996R servo (body 40.7 x 19.7 x 42.9 mm, tabs 53.6 mm long with mounting holes 49.5 mm apart, output shaft up) on top of a 2020 aluminium extrusion at least 300 mm long. The servo drops straight down into a pocket in a printed block with 0.3 mm clearance per side, its tabs rest on the top of the block, and four M4 socket-head bolts fix it through the tab holes (two holes per tab, 49.5 mm apart along the tab and 10 mm apart across it). The block stands on a flange that is bolted into the T-slot of the extrusion by two M5 bolts and T-nuts, and two gussets stiffen the block against a servo torque reaction of 1.5 N m. Show the load path from the servo to the extrusion bolts. It is printed in PLA on an FDM printer without supports.
