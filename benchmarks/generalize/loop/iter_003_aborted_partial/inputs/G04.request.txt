A two-part case for a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, a 65 x 30 mm board. The board is held at its four 2.75 mm mounting holes, on the 58 x 23 mm pattern 3.5 mm in from the edges, by screws into printed standoffs. With the lid on, cables must plug in through openings in the walls, each with room for the plug body: the mini-HDMI port and the two micro-USB ports (data and power), which are all on one long side of the board, and the microSD card slot on a short end, so the card can be pushed in and out. The case is a base and a lid that goes on with M2.5 screws. It is printed on an FDM printer.
