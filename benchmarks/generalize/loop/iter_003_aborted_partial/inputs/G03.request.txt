A box for an ESP32-DevKitC V4 board (54.4 x 27.9 mm, 1.6 mm thick, pin headers pointing up about 8.5 mm). The board has no mounting holes, so the box must hold it with ribs or clips instead of screws. The micro-USB end must be open for a cable, so a micro-USB plug can be pushed in with the lid on. The antenna end of the board needs 8 mm of empty space beyond the board and no wall or lid material above the module. The lid is a separate printed part that snaps on and can be taken off again. Printed on an FDM printer in PLA without supports, and the closed box should be no larger than 75 x 45 x 25 mm.
