A small control box for an Arduino Nano with an Alps EC11 rotary encoder (7 mm threaded bushing, 6 mm shaft) on the top panel, and a small 0.96 inch SSD1306 OLED display behind a window in the same panel, next to the encoder. The top panel is 3 mm thick, and the encoder's nut must seat flat on the panel with the bushing's thread projecting past the nut. The Nano's mini-USB port must be reachable through the back end of the box with the lid on. The top panel is the removable lid, held on by four M3 self-tapping screws; the box is a base plus that lid, printed on an FDM printer.
