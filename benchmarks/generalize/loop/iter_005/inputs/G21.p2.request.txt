It goes straight down into a pocket in a printed block with 0.3 mm to spare on each side, and the ears sit on the upper surface of the block. The block is mounted on a flange, and that flange is fastened in the extrusion's T-slot with two M5 bolts and T-nuts. Add two gussets so the block can take a servo torque reaction of 1.5 N m, and lay out the load path running from the servo down to the extrusion bolts. Print in PLA on an FDM machine without supports.
