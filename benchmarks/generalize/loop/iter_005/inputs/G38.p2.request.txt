I want a holder that tips back and forth so I can solder a 70 x 50 mm perfboard upside down, with the components facing down. Each corner of the board has a 3 mm hole whose centre is 2.5 mm from the two nearest edges, and four pins at the corners go through them. Below the board, where the component bodies dangle, nothing may be within 10 mm, and the soldering face must be open to the top. The board lies in a cradle that swings on two M3 bolts through the uprights of a heavy base, with at least 20 degrees of swing in each direction. That base has to be weighty enough that nothing tips over at a 20 degree tilt, and I print it flat on an FDM printer with no supports.
