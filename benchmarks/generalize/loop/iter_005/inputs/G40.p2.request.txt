I need a drilling template, flat, for the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 hole pattern in a panel. The Pico 2's four mounting holes are 2.1 mm and form a 47.0 x 11.4 mm rectangle (47.0 mm apart lengthwise, 11.4 mm across). Each of the four gets a 2.3 mm guide hole in the template, so a 2.1 mm drill goes through with some play. The template registers against a straight panel edge: I hold it to the edge of the panel, a flat sheet 3 mm thick, and drill. The closest row of holes has to be 10 mm from that edge; I slide it along the edge by eye, so just the distance from the edge and the angle count. In the top face there is a pocket in the shape of the Pico's outline, so I can set a Pico in it to verify placement before I drill. Hole centres have to land within 0.1 mm of the pattern. I print it in PLA on an FDM printer.
