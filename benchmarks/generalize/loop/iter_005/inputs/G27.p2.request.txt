I want a single FDM-printed PLA part that carries an LM8UU linear bearing (8 x 15 x 24 mm) along a 200 mm long, 8 mm smooth rod. Its top plate is flat and 40 x 30 mm, with four M3 holes in a 30 x 20 mm pattern; the bearing housing hangs under the plate, so the top stays flat, and M3 nuts are trapped in pockets on the plate's underside. The rod follows the plate's 30 mm side. Fasteners are 10 mm long ISO 4762 M3 socket-head screws. 3.0 x 1.2 mm zip ties in grooves around the block hold the bearing in, together with a 0.1 mm press fit in its bore: the housing bore is the bearing's 15 mm outside diameter plus the usual 0.1 mm printing allowance, because printed holes shrink by about that much. With the bearing seated, the rod has to pass through without touching the block.
