I want a buzzer alarm housing containing an Arduino Nano and a 12 mm piezo buzzer. In the base, the Nano rests on its four mounting holes with the mini-USB port at one of the short ends; leave that end open, so a mini-USB plug can be inserted and the lid can still be slid off while the cable is attached. Each long wall of the base carries a dovetail rail, and the lid slides onto them along the long axis with 0.25 mm of fit clearance until it butts against the far end wall. Over the buzzer the lid has a ring of sound holes, but the buzzer's own sound port mustn't be blocked. Print in PLA on an FDM printer, no supports; the closed box must stay within 80 x 40 x 30 mm.
