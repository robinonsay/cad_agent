I'd like a rugged-ish little sensor node box, and I'm keeping it weather-resistant within what a printed box can do, meaning it stays sealed except for the USB opening, the switch slot and the seam around the door. Inside goes a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 and one 18650 Li-ion cell (18.6 mm across, 65.2 mm long). The cell lies in a printed cradle along the box, and the bottom has a sliding door that keeps it in place, so I can change the cell without touching the electronics. The Pico is mounted on top of the cell with four M2 screws. For the power switch I'm using an SK12D-type slide switch whose handle sticks out of a side wall, and the Pico's micro-USB port should be reachable through an end wall while the lid is on. Aim for roughly 0.3 mm of clearance per side on the door. Everything is FDM printed without supports: a base, a lid and the door.
