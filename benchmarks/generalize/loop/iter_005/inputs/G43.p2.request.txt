This is for joining two pipes, a 40 mm one and a 50 mm one, with a reducer. On the 40 mm side I want a socket that slips over the outside of the pipe (outside diameter 40 mm); on the 50 mm side a spigot that goes into the inside of the pipe (inside diameter 50 mm). Put a shoulder stop on each side so a pipe end bottoms out once it is 15 mm in. The 40 mm pipe has a 2 mm wall and the 50 mm pipe a 3 mm wall. Make the reducer's own wall 2.4 mm, and give both joints a friction fit with roughly 0.15 mm of interference. I'll print it vertically on an FDM printer with the axis upright, so it has to work without supports.
