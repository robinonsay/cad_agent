The six nozzle pockets are 6 to 10 mm deep and laid out in a regular row or grid, centres within 0.1 mm of the lattice, with at least 3 mm between neighbours so you can pick the nozzles out one at a time with your fingers. When loaded, the centre of mass has to be at least 2 mm inside the four magnets (PLA 1.24 g/cm3, nozzles 3 g each, wrench 45 g, magnets 0.64 g each). Print it flat on an FDM printer with no supports, and keep it no larger than 140 x 40 x 15 mm.
