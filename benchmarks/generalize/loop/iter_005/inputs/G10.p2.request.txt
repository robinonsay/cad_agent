I'm making a little controller housing around an Arduino Nano. Up top there's an Alps EC11 rotary encoder (7 mm threaded bushing, 6 mm shaft) and, right next to it, a 0.96 inch SSD1306 OLED seen through a window in that same panel. That panel is 3 mm thick and doubles as the removable lid, fastened with four M3 self-tapping screws. The encoder nut has to lie flat on the panel with some of the bushing's thread showing beyond the nut. I also need to reach the Nano's mini-USB port through the rear end of the housing while the lid is on. So it's a base and that lid, printed on an FDM printer.
