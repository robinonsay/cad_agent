A knob for an Alps RK09 potentiometer (6 mm D-shaft, 7 mm threaded bushing), 24 mm in diameter and 18 mm tall. The knob locks onto the shaft with an M3 set screw in a radial hole, and the screw's M3 nut is held in a captive hex pocket in the knob. Include the 3 mm thick panel the potentiometer mounts in, with a 7.2 mm hole for the bushing. The knob's skirt must clear the panel by 1 mm. The knob and the panel are two separate FDM printed parts.
