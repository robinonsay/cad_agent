A soldering jig for a Raspberry Pi Pico 2. It holds two 20-pin male headers (2.54 mm pitch, 17.78 mm between the rows) vertical, long pin tails down, while I solder the Pico onto them: the pin guide holes are 1.0 mm, the headers' plastic spacers sit on a flat seat, and the Pico drops on from above onto the pins and rests on a shelf at the right standoff height (the height of the header spacer) with a little room to lift it off afterwards. The jig also has a cradle for a 70 x 50 mm perfboard (1.6 mm thick), a pocket where the board lies flat, with finger notches to lift it out. The jig is one piece printed on an FDM printer in PLA without supports, and I must be able to get a soldering iron to every pin from above.
