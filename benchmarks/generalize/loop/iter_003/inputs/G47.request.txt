A robot-base bracket, printed in one piece, that holds an N20 gearmotor (12 x 10 mm body, 3 mm D shaft) in a clamp pocket and an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor (45 x 20 mm board, two 16 mm transducers at 26 mm pitch) facing forward, 40 mm forward of the motor's shaft axis. A 6 x 3 mm magnet pocket sits at the shaft end for an encoder. Two M3 bolts fix the whole bracket to the chassis. It is printed on an FDM printer without supports, flat on its chassis face.
