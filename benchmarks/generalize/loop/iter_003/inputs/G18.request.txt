A two-piece pillow block for a 608 bearing (8 x 22 x 7 mm) that is clamped, not glued. The block is split along the bearing axis into a base and a cap. Two M3 bolts with captive hex nuts pull the halves together, and the bearing pocket is about 0.1 mm under 22 mm across when the halves are closed, so the clamp holds the bearing. The base has two M4 mounting holes 40 mm apart. It is printed on an FDM printer without supports.
