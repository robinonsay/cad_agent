A hose adapter that joins a hose of 16 mm inner diameter to a pipe of 25 mm outside diameter. The hose end is a barbed spigot with a 16 mm body and three ribs 0.8 mm tall. The pipe end is a socket for the 25 mm pipe, with the bore 0.15 mm bigger than the pipe (0.15 mm loose), 20 mm deep, ending in a stop shoulder for the pipe. The wall is 2 mm throughout. The adapter has a through bore for the flow, an overall length of 60 mm, and a transition between the two sizes that is no steeper than 45 degrees. It must print upright (axis vertical) on an FDM printer in PLA without supports.
