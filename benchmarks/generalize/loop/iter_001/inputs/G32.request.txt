A guard housing over a 20-tooth GT2 pulley (tooth OD 16 mm, flanges 18 mm) on the 5 mm shaft of a NEMA 17 motor (42.3 mm square, M3 holes on a 31 mm square pattern, 22 mm pilot). The pulley hub sits 3 mm above the motor face and the shaft sticks out 24 mm from the face. The guard must clear the pulley and the shaft by 2 mm all around while leaving one slot on one side for the 6 mm GT2 belt, which runs off straight along that side to a second pulley 100 mm away. It is fastened to the motor with M3 bolts in two of the motor's four M3 face holes, and it is printed in PLA on an FDM printer without supports.
