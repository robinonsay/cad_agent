A desktop console for a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W (65 x 30 mm, four 2.75 mm holes on a 58 x 23 mm pattern). A 40 x 40 x 10 mm fan sits above the Pi, fixed on its 32 mm hole pitch and blowing down onto it. A 0.96 inch SSD1306 OLED (27.3 x 27.8 mm, viewing area 25 x 14 mm) shows through a window in the front panel, with an EC11 rotary encoder beside it on the same panel. Both micro-USB ports of the Pi are exposed through the rear wall for cables. There are vents in the side walls, and the heat of the Pi must have a path out through them. The case is a base and a lid that closes with snap latches, printed on an FDM printer without supports. The mini-HDMI and the microSD slot are not used.
